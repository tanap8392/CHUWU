Enter your birth date and time (as precisely as you know it) to calculate your Bazi (八字) — your Four Pillars of Destiny — and see which of the Five Elements your chart could use more of.

Bazi hour pillars are traditionally read in 12 two-hour blocks (时辰) rather than the exact minute, so pick the block you were born in. Not sure? Leave it on the midday estimate — your Day and Year pillars will still be accurate; only the Hour pillar will be approximate. Place of birth refines the reading using true solar time.
